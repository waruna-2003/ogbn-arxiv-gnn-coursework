On the OGBN-Arxiv node classification task (169,343 papers, 2,315,598 citation edges, 40 classes), our GCN model achieved 71.71% test accuracy (macro-F1 0.5054) and our GAT model achieved 70.72% test accuracy (macro-F1 0.4781). GCN produced the higher macro-F1 score, suggesting its simpler, fixed-weight neighbourhood aggregation generalizes at least as well as learned attention on this dataset.
